Othello

Play classic 8x8 Reversi in your browser.

Turn: Black
Black: 2 White: 2

How to play

  • Black moves first. Click a highlighted square to place a disk.
  • Any opponent disks trapped between your new disk and another of your disks flip.
  • If a player has no legal moves, their turn is passed.
  • The game ends when neither player can move or the board is full.
